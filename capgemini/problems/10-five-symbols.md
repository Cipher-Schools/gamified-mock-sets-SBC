# C10: Five symbols

**Type:** Deductive symbol grid  
**Level:** Medium  
**Practice time:** 1–2 minutes (a practice target, not an official limit)

Each row and each column must contain **A, B, C, D, E** exactly once. A dot is a missing symbol. Fill every dot. There is **no smaller-box rule**.

```text
    1 2 3 4 5
 1  A . C D E
 2  B . D . A
 3  . D E A B
 4  D E . B .
 5  E . B C D
```

Write your answer before opening the solution.
