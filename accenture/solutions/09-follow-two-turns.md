# A09: Follow two turns — solution

**Answer:** 7 moves, including the move off the board.

Follow this route:

`R2C1 → R2C2 → R2C3 → R2C4 → R3C4 → R4C4 → R5C4 → outside`

There are 7 cell-to-cell moves. The last one leaves the board.

**Watch out:** Returning to a cell is only a loop if the direction is also the same.
