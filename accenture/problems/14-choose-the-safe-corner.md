# A14: Choose the safe corner

**Type:** Set the path  
**Level:** Medium  
**Practice time:** 90 seconds (a practice target, not an official limit)

The token starts at **R4C5**, facing **L**. Put one arrow (U, D, L or R) in each `?` cell before it starts. It keeps moving straight on dots and turns as soon as it reaches an arrow. Stop when it reaches E. It must stay inside the board until then.

```text
    1 2 3 4 5 6
 1  . . . . . .
 2  . E . . . .
 3  . . . . . .
 4  . ? . ? S .
 5  . . . . . .
```

Which arrows lead to E? How many moves does the token make? Each move goes to a neighbouring cell. The start counts as zero.

Write your answer before opening the solution.
