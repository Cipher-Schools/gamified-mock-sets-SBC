# A19: Pick the key order

**Type:** Keys and doors  
**Level:** Hard  
**Practice time:** 2–4 minutes (a practice target, not an official limit)

Start at S. Collect every lowercase key, then enter E. Move up, down, left or right by one cell per move. `#` is a wall. Dots are open cells. You may revisit cells. E stays blocked until all keys have been collected.

An uppercase A or B is a locked door: pick up the matching lowercase key before entering it. Keys are kept and are never used up.

```text
    1 2 3 4 5 6
 1  S . . . . a
 2  . # # . # .
 3  b . . # E .
 4  . # # . # .
 5  . . . . c .
```

What is the fewest number of moves? Give one route using U, D, L and R.

Write your answer before opening the solution.
