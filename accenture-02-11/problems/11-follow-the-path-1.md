# 11. Follow the path 1

Start at **S**, moving right. Each move goes one cell. On U, D, L or R, turn up, down, left or right. On a dot, keep going straight.

```text
S . R . .
. . . . .
. . D . .
. . . . .
. . . . .
```

Does the token leave the grid or loop? If it leaves, how many moves does it make? Count the final move outside. Rows and columns start at 1.
