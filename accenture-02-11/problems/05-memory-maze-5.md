# 5. Memory maze 5

Open the map below for 10 seconds, then close it. Remember the walls, key and exit.

Start at S, collect K, then reach E. E is blocked until you have K. Move one cell up, down, left or right. A # is a wall and a dot is an open cell. You may revisit cells.

<details>
<summary>Open the map</summary>

```text
  1 2 3 4 5
1 E . S . .
2 . . . # .
3 . # # . .
4 . . # K .
5 . # . . .
```

</details>

Close the map and write a shortest route using U, D, L and R. How many moves does it take?
