# 11. Set the arrow 1

A token starts at S, facing right. Put one arrow U, D, L or R in the `?` cell. The token moves one cell at a time, turns when it reaches an arrow, and continues straight on dots. It stops at E. U means up, D down, L left and R right.

```text
  1 2 3 4 5
1 S ? . . .
2 . . . . U
3 . . . . .
4 . E . . .
5 . . . . .
```

Which arrow reaches E? How many moves does it take? Count each move to the next cell; the start is zero.
