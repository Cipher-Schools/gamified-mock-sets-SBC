# 22. Key route 2

Start at S, collect K, then reach E. The exit is blocked until you collect the key. A # is a wall; a dot is an open cell. Move one cell up, down, left or right. You can revisit cells.

```text
. . . . S
. . # # #
K . . # .
. . # # E
. . . . .
```

What is the fewest moves? Give one route using U, D, L and R.
